01 — MEET BEX

Training should make your life better.

Stronger for the things you actually want to do.

Hi, I’m Bex Haywood. I help people build strength, fitness and confidence through training that is practical, enjoyable and built around real life.

I don’t believe exercise should feel like punishment. I want you to enjoy what you are doing, feel confident in your body, and have a clear sense that the work you put in is taking you somewhere.

My own background has always been driven by sport — first swimming, then bodybuilding, and finally powerlifting. Powerlifting suits me because progress is measurable. You can track your strength, hold yourself accountable and experience the buzz of achieving something you once thought was out of reach.

That feeling is exactly why I do what I do. I love helping other people set a goal, work towards it, and realise they are capable of more than they expected.

02 — MY APPROACH

Three things guide every session.

The best training plan is one you can enjoy, sustain and progress with.

01

Enjoyment

Is it fun? Do you actually like what you are doing? Do you look forward to it in your routine? If the answer is yes, you are far more likely to stay consistent.

02

Injury prevention & management

You shouldn’t simply have to accept pain. Training should help you move better, build resilience and do everything practical to minimise injury now and in the future.

03

Achieving goals

Big or small, short-term or long-term — goals create direction. With the right plan, support and consistency, meaningful progress is possible.

“The buzz you get from smashing a goal is too good not to encourage.”

— Bex Haywood
